Click Counter is a small, persistent click-counter game written in **roblox-ts**, powered by **React**, **Lyra**, **Flamework**, and previewed with **UI Labs**. It exists mainly so you can pop the hood and see how these pieces fit together in a real (if tiny) project — the game itself is just an excuse.

---

## The game

- Click **Click** to increment your counter
- Click **Reset** to zero it out
- Thanks to Lyra, your count is saved — leave and come back, and you're right where you left off
